LAS-era publication figures and tables

Updated October 15, 2026. Final cohort: 1,007,801 candidate-organ observations, including 42,874 lung candidates.

Lung listings and follow-up end March 8, 2023; other organs retain their original study periods. Lung time-varying models use official calculated LAS updated at monthly interval starts; CAS is not used. National AJ quartile thresholds retain the original reference-cohort definition.

Subgroup estimates marked * have convergence warnings involving the age-under-12 nuisance term. See the model log before interpretation. Map values are model-implied net risks from cause-specific Cox models, not competing-risk cumulative incidence.

This index identifies the current publication displays. Earlier full-era lung proxy analyses, reconstructed LAS/CAS experiments, and obsolete waitlist-average/KM figures remain historical and are not included here. The 2015-2019 three-year competing-risk sensitivity is unchanged because its follow-up ends before the LAS transition.
