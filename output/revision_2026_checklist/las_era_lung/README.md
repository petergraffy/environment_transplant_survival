# LAS-era lung analysis

## Scope

Lung candidates listed through March 8, 2023 are retained. Follow-up is also
administratively censored on March 8, 2023, before the March 9 LAS-to-CAS
transition. CAS is not used. Other organs are unchanged. These are versioned
replacement lung results; existing manuscript figures and canonical tables
have NOT yet been regenerated from this bundle.

There are 42,874 retained candidates, 9,739 excluded later listings, and 1,274
follow-up periods truncated at the transition. There are 5,545 composite adverse
outcomes, including 3,287 waitlist deaths, before model-specific exclusions.
Original primary candidate consolidation, first listing center, and terminal
outcome definitions are preserved.

## LAS measurement

Official CANHX_LAS_SCORE_CALC is linked using PX_ID and
CANHX_LAS_SCORE_BEGIN_DT from the new Lung_supplement2603 source. Match LAS and
CAS are not substituted. Eligible scores must occur during the source
registration and no later than the candidate's end of observation. When
different centers update scores on the same day, the original first-listed
registration is preferred, then the lowest PX_ID. Within-registration same-day
conflicting scores would be omitted; none were found.

At each existing monthly analysis interval start, the latest available LAS is
carried forward. Later scores are never backfilled. Thus this is monthly-updated
LAS, not a model split at every daily score change. LAS is unavailable below
age 12. Missing values are filled with the fixed positive baseline LAS median
(37.80857), with missing-LAS and under-12 indicators. Recorded zeros are retained.
Baseline LAS is observed for 41,853 candidates; median 37.7 (IQR, 33.9-45.6).
Observed LAS covers 97.8% of candidate follow-up days before model exclusions.

## Models

Baseline: prior-365-day PM2.5/O3 or prior-12-month NO2, adjusted for age, sex,
race, ACS vulnerability proxy, and transplant-center strata; no LAS adjustment.

Time-varying: monthly interval exposure, age, sex, race, ACS vulnerability proxy,
LAS per 10 points, missing-LAS and under-12 indicators, and center strata.
The original monthly exposure construction and annual NO2 fallback are retained.
Neither primary model adds listing year. Efron ties and model-based standard
errors are used, without person clustering. Complete-case covariate and exposure
eligibility is applied. Subgroup formulas are explicitly recorded in model_log.csv.

| Exposure increment | Baseline HR (95% CI) | Time-varying HR (95% CI) |
|---|---|---|
| PM2.5, 5 ug/m3 | 1.13 (1.05-1.21) | 1.09 (1.04-1.14) |
| NO2, 10 ppb | 1.08 (1.02-1.13) | 1.15 (1.09-1.21) |
| O3, 10 ppb | 0.99 (0.91-1.08) | 0.98 (0.96-1.01) |

Each baseline primary model has 40,721 candidates and 5,206 events; each
time-varying primary model has 42,254 candidates and 5,468 events. The previous
proxy on the same restricted time-varying cohort gives HRs of 1.15 (1.10-1.20)
for PM2.5 and 1.26 (1.19-1.33) for NO2. This comparison isolates severity
adjustment from the change in cohort period.

## Outputs and checks

- pollutant_results.csv: primary, multipollutant, subgroup, and sensitivity HRs.
- full_coefficients.csv: all covariate coefficients and their uncertainty.
- model_log.csv: exact formulas, denominators, events, and warnings.
- cohort_and_las_audit.csv: exclusion and score linkage counts.
- baseline_las_summary.csv: baseline LAS descriptive statistics.
- outcome_components.csv: separate outcome component counts.
- time_updated_las_coverage.csv: score coverage over follow-up.

All 97 fits produced estimates. All six primary fits are warning-free. Three
time-varying Asian subgroup fits have a possible infinite-coefficient warning
for the under-12 indicator; those exploratory subgroup fits require caution
and are not certified as fully converged. Warnings are preserved in each output.
